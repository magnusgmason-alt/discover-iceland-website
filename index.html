About the guide

One island.
Every corner.

Iceland is small enough to drive around in a week and wild enough to fill a lifetime. Discover Iceland is a complete travel guide to the island: when to go, how to get around, where to stay, what to eat, what it costs, how to drive safely, and how to read the weather.

Fire & ice

Built by volcanoes. Carved by ice.

Iceland sits on the Mid-Atlantic Ridge, where the North American and Eurasian plates pull apart, so it is still being made. Glaciers cover about a tenth of the island, the Reykjanes peninsula near the airport has erupted again and again since 2021, and hot water from underground heats about nine in ten homes.

The seasonal editions

Midnight sun or winter dark.

In June the sun barely sets and the highland roads open. In December there are four hours of daylight, the roads can close with snow, and the sky comes alive at night. Each season gets its own book, with routes, gear and safety for that time of year.
