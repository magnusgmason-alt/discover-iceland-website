Fire & ice

Built by volcanoes. Carved by ice.

Iceland sits on the Mid-Atlantic Ridge, where the North American and Eurasian plates pull apart, so it is still being made. Glaciers cover about a tenth of the island, the Reykjanes peninsula near the airport has erupted again and again since 2021, and hot water from underground heats about nine in ten homes.

Northern lights

When the sky comes alive.

From about September to April, clear and dark nights can light up green over the whole island. You need three things: darkness, a clear sky and solar activity. The Icelandic Met Office publishes a cloud and aurora forecast every evening, and a few nights away from town lights give you the best chance.
